C Calio

See your real calorie balance

A calorie tracker for iPhone that keeps your data on your phone.

Calio shows what you have eaten against what you have actually burned, instead of just a shrinking daily budget. Exercise is counted once — never double-counted — and you can tap any number on the dashboard to see the formula and the inputs behind it.

What it does

  • Guided setup computes a personalised calorie target, with a safety floor so it never drops below a healthy minimum.
  • Fast logging: search, recents, favourites, barcode scanning, or a custom food in seconds.
  • Exercise logging with MET-based estimates you can override.
  • Weight tracking with a smoothed trend line and goal progress.
  • Optional Apple Health sync for workouts and weight.
  • Optional AI logging: photograph a meal or describe it in a sentence.

Privacy

Calio has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your logs, weight and profile stay on your iPhone. The privacy policy sets out exactly what the one optional AI feature sends, and what it does not.
