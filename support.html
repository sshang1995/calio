Common questions

Why doesn’t my exercise increase my calorie budget twice?

Because it shouldn’t. Your activity level already accounts for everyday movement. Workouts you log are added on top of that once, and only once — which is the whole reason Calio exists.

Where does my data live?

On your iPhone. Calio has no accounts and no cloud sync: your logs, weight and profile stay in the app. See the privacy policy for the detail, including the one optional feature that sends anything off the device.

How do I export or delete everything?

Open the Profile tab and use the data controls there. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your device permanently.

Apple Health isn’t syncing

Open the Health app, go to Sharing › Apps › Calio, and confirm the categories you want are switched on. iOS asks for read and write permission separately, and it will not re-prompt after the first time — permissions have to be changed in Health or in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

What does Calio Premium include?

The AI food-logging features: photographing a meal, or describing it in a sentence, and having the foods and portions estimated for you. Everything else in Calio is free.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Free trials must be cancelled at least 24 hours before they end to avoid being charged. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The AI feature says it’s unavailable

Check that you have an active subscription and a working internet connection, then try again. If it keeps failing, email us with the rough time it happened so we can look into it.

Health disclaimer

Calio provides estimates for general wellness purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare provider before making significant changes to your diet or exercise.
