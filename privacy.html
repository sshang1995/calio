Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026

The short version. Calio keeps your food logs, weight, exercise and profile on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us, and we have no account system, no analytics and no advertising. The only things that ever leave your device are the words you type into food search, and the photo or sentence you submit to the optional AI food-logging feature — and we keep neither.

Who we are

Calio is an independent iOS app. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the developer of Calio, and “the app” means the Calio iOS application. Your use of the app is also governed by the Terms of Use (EULA).

What stays on your device

Almost everything. The following is written to a private database inside the app on your iPhone and is never transmitted to us or to anyone else:

  • Your profile: age, sex, height, weight, activity level and goal.
  • Everything you log: foods, meals, portions, exercise and weight entries.
  • Your calculated targets, daily totals and progress history.
  • Custom foods and favourites you create.

Because this data never reaches a server, we cannot see it, we cannot recover it for you, and it disappears when you delete the app. You can also export or erase it yourself at any time from within the app.

What leaves your device

Four things, and only in the circumstances described.

1. Optional AI food logging

If you use “Snap a Photo” or “Describe It”, the app sends what you submitted to Calio’s own server, which forwards it to Anthropic’s Claude API so it can be read and turned into an estimated food entry.

  • What is sent: the meal photo you took or chose, or the food description you typed or dictated, plus which meal you are logging it to.
  • What is not sent: your name, email, account, device identifier, location, weight, profile or logging history. None of it is attached to the request.
  • Retention: our server processes the request and returns the result without writing it to any database or log. Anthropic’s handling of API data is governed by their own terms; at the time of writing Anthropic does not train its models on API inputs.
  • Avoiding it entirely: these two features are optional. Barcode scanning is read on your device, and foods you enter by hand or pick from your own recents and favourites never leave it.

2. Food search

When you search for a food, the words you type are sent to Calio’s server, which passes the query on to USDA FoodData Central — a public nutrition database run by the US Department of Agriculture — and returns the matching foods.

  • What is sent: the search text itself, for example “oatmeal”.
  • What is not sent: your name, email, account, device identifier, location, weight, profile or logging history. The query is not attached to you in any way.
  • Retention: our server passes the query through and returns the result without writing it to any database or log.
  • Why it goes through us: so the database key stays on our server instead of being embedded in the app, where anyone could extract and misuse it.

3. Subscription checks

Calio Premium is sold through Apple. When the app calls the AI feature, it attaches the signed receipt Apple issued for your subscription so our server can confirm the subscription is genuine and active before doing the work.

That receipt is generated and signed by Apple. It identifies the purchase — a transaction identifier and which product was bought — not you. It carries no name, email or payment details, and we never receive your payment information: Apple handles the transaction, and Apple’s own privacy policy covers it.

4. Nothing else

There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracker anywhere in the app. The app contains no third-party code beyond Apple’s own frameworks. We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Calio can read your workouts and body-weight entries to save you logging them twice, and can write back weights and workouts you record in Calio so your other health apps stay in sync.

This exchange happens entirely on your device, between Calio and Apple Health. Health data is never transmitted to our server, and it is never used for advertising, sold, or shared with third parties. You choose which categories to allow, and you can withdraw permission at any time in the Health app under Sharing › Apps, or in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security › Health.

Camera, photos, microphone and speech

  • Camera — used to scan product barcodes and to photograph a meal. A barcode is read on your device. A meal photo is only sent anywhere if you go on to submit it to the AI feature described above.
  • Photo library — only the specific image you pick is read, and only when you pick it.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — used for dictation, so you can describe a food by speaking. Calio requires on-device speech recognition, so your speech audio is transcribed on your iPhone and the audio is not sent to Apple or to us. The resulting text is treated exactly like text you typed.

Children

Calio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app has no sign-up, so we hold no personal information about any user.

Your rights

Because your data lives on your own device rather than on our servers, you already hold it directly: you can view, export or delete all of it from within the app, and deleting the app removes it. If you are in a jurisdiction with statutory rights of access or erasure — for example the UK/EU GDPR or the CCPA — there is in practice no personal data of yours in our possession to produce or erase, since we store none. You are welcome to contact us to confirm this.

Health disclaimer

Calio provides estimates for general wellness purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare provider before making significant changes to your diet or exercise.
